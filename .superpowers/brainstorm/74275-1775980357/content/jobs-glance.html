The Jobs details

Staggered entrance. Names don't appear all at once. They float in one by one, 120ms apart, each rising from nothing and settling. Like curtains opening to reveal who's waiting for you.

Urgency = size. Most urgent name is 34px. Least urgent is 24px with 65% opacity. Your eye reads priority before your brain processes text. Size IS the ranking.

The glow responds to proximity. Before you tap, as your finger approaches, the glow under that name blooms. Others recede. The atmosphere shifts toward that name's accent. The screen anticipates your intent.

The morph. Tap a name. The name GROWS. Glass materializes around it. The context line expands into the full message. The reply bar slides up. It's not a page transition — the name BECOMES the FocusCard. Same element. Continuous motion.

Atmosphere = load. 5 names = 5 warm blobs in the mesh, active breathing. 1 name = 1 gentle glow. Zero names = nearly invisible cool wash. The background color temperature of the screen is the count before you read a word.
