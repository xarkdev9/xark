The screen IS your state.

You open the app. In 0.5 seconds, before reading a single word, the visual density of the screen tells you your morning. Not a list. Not a dashboard. The screen itself mirrors your social obligations.

3 approaches. Same principle. Different physics.

IDEA 1
The Adaptive Mosaic
One screen. No scrolling. Cards reshape themselves based on how many things need you. 1 item = full-bleed hero. 3 items = hero + two medium. 6 items = dense mosaic. The layout IS the urgency signal. Open the app, glance, know your morning.
